## Why Free Dictionary?

Most dictionary apps open a connection before they open a definition.
**Free Dictionary never does.** The entire dataset ships inside the APK —
108,000+ English entries, 14,800+ idioms, 36,000+ IPA transcriptions — so
every lookup is instant, private, and available on a plane, in a basement,
or on a dead zone.

And it doesn't just define words — it shows how they *live*: the idioms they
appear in, the prepositions they pair with, the phrasal verbs they build,
the words they get confused with, and how to pronounce every one of them.

## Features

- 📴 **Fully offline** — the whole dictionary is bundled; lookups never
  touch a network
- 🔍 **Instant search** with live suggestions across 108,000+ entries
- 🗣️ **Idioms** — 14,800+ records, every one with a usage example
- 🔤 **Pronunciation (IPA)** — US/UK text transcriptions for 36,000+ words,
  including inflected forms
- 🪞 **Homographs** — same spelling, different meaning & pronunciation,
  each reading mapped to its meaning
- 🧩 **Usage patterns** — prepositions, phrasal verbs & collocations
  (43,500+ records) with side-by-side comparison, plus verb forms
- 🔗 **Verified word relations** — synonyms, antonyms, homophones, related
  terms; all resolve to real entries
- 🕘 **Smart history** — recency, most-searched, faved, saved & A–Z filters
- 📚 **Favorites & named collections** — multi-select, move between
  collections, create on the fly
- 🌗 **Light & dark themes** — persisted, system default respected
- ✍️ **Selectable text** — long-press any definition or example to copy
- 🚫 **No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no permissions**

## FAQ

**Is it *really* offline?**
Yes. The dictionary dataset is an asset inside the app. There is no server,
no API call, and no download step — first launch to millionth lookup.

**Does it collect anything?**
No. No analytics, no ads, no accounts, no tracking SDKs. Your history,
favorites and collections live only on your device.

**Where does the data come from?**
A Wiktionary-derived dataset, heavily refined by a custom enrichment
pipeline: idioms mined with examples, IPA pronunciations curated per accent,
homograph readings mapped to meanings, and 30,000+ broken word-relation
references pruned.

**iOS version?**
Not yet — Android first.

**Is the source code available?**
The app is closed-source while in active development. This repository is the
showcase: downloads, screenshots and release notes. If the source ever goes
public, it will be announced here.
